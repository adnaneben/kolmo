  Description     : structure-aware lossless compressor

 Kolmo is a lossless compressor that detects the structure of its input
 (columns, records, numeric fields, images, audio) and chooses a model for
 each part. It has levels 1 (fastest) to 9 (max) and an ultra mode,
 random-access reads of a byte range, and decoding that scales across cores.

 Binary packages: kolmo (command line), libkolmo0 (shared library),
 libkolmo-dev (header, pkg-config file).

 Why it is useful: on tables, logs and scientific arrays Kolmo produces
 smaller output than general-purpose compressors at comparable speeds (the
 upstream benchmark pages give the measurements and the method). Its frame
 format is specified (FORMAT.md) and upstream promises that every release
 reads every frame written since 0.0.1 (COMPATIBILITY.md).

 Related packages: zstd, xz-utils, brotli, bzip2 and lz4 are general-purpose
 compressors; none of them models the structure of the input. Kolmo links no
 third-party codec library; it includes ported code from libbsc and a subset
 of libsais (both by Ilya Grebnov, Apache-2.0), which are not packaged in
 Debian as libraries.
