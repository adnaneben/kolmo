# Same-preset PCM16 routing experiment

The research option evaluates the existing KAUD specialist (wire codec 19) early at unmodified L7 or Ultra. On acceptance it emits the normal candidate payload through the ordinary frame writer. Decode uses stock v0.0.2. All public levels, libraries and the frame format stay on the pinned main behavior unless the research macro and environment switch are both enabled. Pinned control: a1d39559686a9ec17c8614b9154a64d7b5b98672. Retained source: d6435c9a.

## Results and decision

Keep the conservative implementation opt-in for further qualification. At five scored randomized pairs per cell, 24 of 88 settings activated on six inputs: three environmental ESC-50 clips, Pydub test4.wav and two CMU PocketSphinx speech clips. Every one of those 120 paired comparisons improved combined throughput. Median paired speedup ranged from 2.16× to 9.45× across the 24 cells. The six recordings cover three source collections; four settings per recording represent two presets and requested thread counts. All 88 same-preset settings emitted fully byte-identical frames to pinned main. All 1,144 readbacks including warmups and disabled controls reproduced the source with the released v0.0.2 reader. The decoder itself is unchanged.

On the three source-disjoint Pydub/PocketSphinx clips, T1 combined speedups ranged from 3.21× to 5.22×. Pydub test4.wav at L7 stayed at **232,286 bytes**, with encoder time falling from **218.21 ms to 29.92 ms**. Released-reader phase medians were **13.05 ms and 13.23 ms**. The medians are descriptive: paired speedup is the median of per-pair combined-throughput ratios, not a ratio of sums of phase medians.

The conservative rule requires full PCM16 WAV, a single 64 KiB–2 MiB block, small non-audio head/tail, and the default full-analysis L7 or Ultra preset. Four distributed windows must resist quick LZ, and the specialist must produce a moderate-size payload. All other inputs continue through the ordinary candidate comparison. Fallback has a real probe cost: across 64 non-activating cells median paired speedup per cell ranged from 0.76× to 1.36× (median 1.002×). See the isolated A/A/B retest below. This is shared-host Apple M5 evidence. Independent x86 data and wider file sizes and audio producers remain open.

## Experiments retained as negative controls

A broader rule accepted two moderately complex windows combined with a full-block KLZ margin. The three speech/PCM transfer inputs still matched, but stereo Frog grew 304 bytes at L7 and 1,568 bytes at Ultra. Narrowing that alternative to mono allowed Purr to grow from 10,226 to 15,480 bytes at L7 and from 9,860 to 14,415 bytes at Ultra. Blow also grew in mono. Reject both broadenings. The original conservative rule, frozen before the expanded transfers, safely fell back on all these inputs. Recorded alternatives are native-source diffs, applicable sequentially on the original conservative variant; mirror files into the bindings only when rebuilding those separate experiments.

## Whole-file competition

Tested FLAC 1.5.0 -5 and -8 with no padding or seektable and preservation of foreign WAV metadata. All competitor decoded files matched the complete original byte stream. On the 768,044-byte Pydub recording at T1 the conservative Kolmo L7 result was **232,286 bytes at 16.00 MB/s**, Ultra **223,523 at 6.13 MB/s**, FLAC -5 **240,891 at 33.37 MB/s**, and FLAC -8 **238,048 at 30.77 MB/s**. The Kolmo settings occupied less space, while FLAC finished the round trip faster. All 16 workload/arm rows plus phase medians are in frozen-flac/flac-score.csv. The separate rejected-flac/ directory measures the discarded mono alternative and cannot support claims about the retained option.

## Replay and inputs

The candidate needs the ordinary make build plus `CFLAGS='-O3 -mcpu=native -DKOLMO_AUDIO_SCREEN'` on Apple arm64 and `KOLMO_AUDIO_SCREEN=1` on encoding. The frozen release reader was built from tag v0.0.2, commit 550c02fd89d00b767ca540b117301c185dbbb6c9. SHA-256s are b67bbddb88d78d26313ae9ec11718e4cfb2e546f2aa0fd75faa36ee95ad9106f (main), bc6172ff44d7da17696bca5f98f9f2e0bef56e0824c0f8512018fa441142081c (retained candidate) and e7f58f11fe2eb5f296bcd71a9ef9331d154723709ca6822e3cb110acd376e328 (released reader). Build flags otherwise follow the stock Makefile including disabled FP contraction. No hash- or filename-based routing occurs.

`compare.py --help` describes the runner. First-stage files are raw.csv and score.csv for ESC-50, Apple stereo and synthetic controls, speech/ for Mozilla/Python speech, and frozen-recheck/ for Pydub, PocketSphinx, JFK and Apple mono. second/ and mono/ record the deliberately disqualified alternatives. Each raw row carries the exact original SHA, full-frame SHA and header, complete frame bytes, requested threads, level, milliseconds per phase and combined decimal MB/s. Run `compare_flac.py` with the four named files to repeat the competitor matrix. `option-controls.csv` verifies ordinary presets 1–6, 8–9, analysis disabled and explicit multiblock behavior with the retained binary.

Use the stable Git blob IDs in protocol.md for Pydub, PocketSphinx, whisper.cpp, SpeechRecognition and DeepSpeech. CMU raw files are wrapped as 16 kHz mono little-endian PCM16 with Python's wave module. Use the named samples and raw SHA for the ESC-50 files. Apple-provided 24-bit AIFFs were converted using `afconvert -f WAVE -d LEI16`, adding `-c 1` only for the named mono cohort. Benchmarks and digests apply to the converted files. Synthetic wave fixtures are recreated by compare.py. The experiment distributes no third-party corpus files.

## Fifteen-triplet confirmation

The same opt-in binary ran A and AA with the route disabled, B enabled, randomizing the three arms and cell order at every repetition. Each cell had one warmup and 15 scored triplets. Every one of the 192 released-reader decodes recovered the source; every triplet had one unique full-frame digest. Pydub test4 at L7/T1 used **80.34% less combined elapsed time and 81.88% less CPU** relative to the two controls, faster in every triplet. The worst initial Purr Ultra/T4 warning reversed to 2.75% less time in the paired retest. Purr L7/T1 took **3.07% more elapsed time** (11/15 slower, 2.29% more CPU). Tink L7/T1 took 2.63% more elapsed time (9/15 slower, 1.09% more CPU). Preserve this fallback cost when deciding whether the option suits a mixed audio collection. See fallback-score.csv for same-binary control movement.

The final implementation additionally rejects an unsuccessful fast LZ probe, such as an allocation failure, before shortcutting. The benchmarked scores above belong to d6435c9a. final-equivalence.csv exercises all 88 retained settings on the hardened version and compares activation, complete emitted frame and released-reader SHA against those measurements. The replay runner accepts source directories and regenerates synthetic data. final-frozen.txt records the independent native frozen-frame corpus run. The result remains an experimental opt-in.
