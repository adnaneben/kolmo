The X-ray 4 MiB prefix also grew under both rules (L7→3: 1,818,682 to 1,823,454; L6→5: 1,820,841 to 1,821,173). The independent GE file favored the graph route. L7→3 grew its 256 KiB prefix from 20,050 to 32,918 bytes (+64.18%); L6→5 grew the complete 527,370-byte file from 30,714 to 32,190 (+4.81%).

Overall L7→3 improved the combined speed–ratio product in 16/16 requested source/size/thread contexts, but increased bytes in 12/16; only the four MR-prefix cases improved both. L6→5 improved the product in 14/16 contexts and increased bytes in 8/16. The global preset and a rule conditional only on the IMG16 transform both fail the Pareto gate.
