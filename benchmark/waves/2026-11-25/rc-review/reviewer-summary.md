### A cheap bound for the small-record lane

A nonempty v12 one-block record carrying the ordinary four plan bytes spends 32 bytes on the header, 16 on the index entry, and four on plan bytes before the coded payload. At a 110-byte input that consumes 47.3% of the original width. Even with a hypothetical zero-byte coded payload, the ratio ceiling is 110/52 ≈ 2.12 in that representation. Batching 40 such records into one block can remove up to 39 × 52 = 2,028 metadata bytes before counting record boundaries, checksums or changes in compressed payload. This is an accounting bound, not a measured gain. A real cohort should measure full bytes, per-record lookup cost and decode-all time together. Source: [wire layout](https://github.com/adnaneben/kolmo/blob/4db0425b/src/core/container.h).

## Outstanding engineering and benchmark coverage

- Keep phase-aware graph intermediates, compressed input buffering, explicit capped workspace and Linux cgroup ancestor accounting visible. Verify actual constrained process RSS separately from API admission limits. Per-member atomic publication and whole-tree transactional extraction have distinct guarantees. Installed release assets, attestations and wheels require a live RC run with verified downloads.
- The first learning milestone has encoder-only prototypes #61 and #78. Production needs immutable catalogue/policy digests, observable decision costs, conservative unknown-format fallback, source-family-disjoint sealed holdouts, shadow evaluation, rollback to an approved policy and ordinary stock v12 decode without side data.
- A fast-codec benchmark cannot settle full archiver performance. Expand direct matched lanes to deep multi-version trees, tiny API calls, sparse/metadata-heavy and incompressible files, random reads and streamed first-prefix latency. Include independent per-member and solid comparisons. Count all bytes and metadata needed for exact reconstruction, admission RSS, cold and warm p95 with enough trials, requested and actual workers, encode/decode wall and CPU separately.
- Priority comparator gaps in the latest measurements include same-host full-size PAQ8PX/cmix, neural archival cost, trained and training-free OpenZL, native ZipNN under identical IO boundaries, and format specialists on fresh files. Historically proposed families include genuinely lossless images/audio/video, real non-Latin text, columnar and scientific formats, reference-dependent genomics, model checkpoints, packages and long-range incremental snapshots. A rival which cannot restore the identical original container may be evaluated on a separately stated semantic lane.
- Isolated independent Linux/x86, another ARM generation and sustained compute/load controls matter before controller or ISA defaults change. Use matched candidate and baseline launch paths; speed from old lzbench, full CLI and selective extraction cannot be pooled. Archive cryptography, distribution licensing and audited releases are separate product prerequisites and have no demonstrated ratio benefit.
- For site-specific decisions, record the nondominated ratio versus paired combined-throughput frontier and a fixed deployment floor. Keep candidate scope small and interpretable. A stable width or thread-specific win can be admitted without forcing faster presets through an expensive probe.
