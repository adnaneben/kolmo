## Measurement boundaries

- **Local measurements:** Apple M5/macOS arm64 for desktop codec timings unless specified. L is the preset, T is the requested worker count. Combined MB/s uses original bytes divided by matched encode-plus-decode time and includes CLI startup when the linked protocol is whole-file CLI. Exact flags, source and frame hashes, repetitions and machine details are in the linked evidence.
- **Mac load:** [#57's report](https://github.com/adnaneben/kolmo/issues/57#issuecomment-7172580599) puts the affected window at approximately 22:00 Riyadh through load recovery. Timings in that window are unreliable. The local speed figures below are **provisional wherever no contemporaneous load record establishes an acceptable run**. Exact bytes, hashes and correctness observations remain usable. The Backblaze/BGL rerun logged one-minute load 6.9–7.4 and the tip benchmark records about 5.9–6.9. The tip all-levels confirmation carries one load sample (5.92), so it does not establish per-trial load. The PAQ restart at 22:01 was inside the affected window, so its eventual elapsed result needs a load-qualified repeat for speed conclusions. New timed runs require one-minute load below 12.
- **Pinned sources:** historical before `4db0425b`, main `a1d39559`, prior RC `a2e430fe`, integrated benchmark #106 `8d23095b`, guarded binding stack #110 `516e1dca`. A local measurement on any of these revisions cannot establish a hosted result on another revision.
