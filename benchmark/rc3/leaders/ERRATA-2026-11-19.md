## E1 (C6): audio against FLAC -8, `RESULTS.md` line 37

Printed: "`ratio` is 8.9% smaller than FLAC -8, `balanced` 4.6%".
Recomputed from the raw rows (`bench/lead/S1.jsonl`, `S10.jsonl`, 40 ESC-50 clips, 17,641,760 input bytes;
space improvement = 100 × (FLAC − Kolmo) / FLAC):

| Kolmo | total bytes | FLAC -8 total | smaller than FLAC -8 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `-m ratio` | 6,537,582 | 7,119,853 | **8.1781%** |
| `-m balanced` | 6,840,066 | 7,119,853 | **3.9297%** |

The printed 8.9% uses Kolmo as the denominator (FLAC is 8.9065% larger than `ratio`). The printed 4.6% is `balanced`
against `ratio`, read off the "vs smallest" column, not a comparison with FLAC. The specialist tables at lines
345–349 and 427–431 are relative to the smallest tool (`kolmo -m ratio`). A denominator correction needs no codec rerun.
Corrected line: "audio: `ratio` is 8.2% smaller than FLAC -8, `balanced` 3.9%, but FLAC decodes 2.3–15× faster".

## E2 (C3): busy-marked trials were not excluded

`run_cli2.py` and the leaders callers record fastest-of-N wall time, peak `ru_maxrss` only (no time-weighted
average), no argv, input hash or binary hash in the rows, and resume by (group, basename, label). Busy flags (a `ps`
sample before and after each setting, processes at ≥ 50% CPU) were recorded but never used to exclude rows.
In `bench/lead/T1-*.jsonl` (one thread): 1,736 trial rows, 328 busy-marked, including all 144 structured rows;
200 of the busy rows name compilers, tests or other codec jobs. Every T1 timing claim in `RESULTS.md` therefore needs a
clean rerun before it is relied on. Compressed byte counts are deterministic and are not invalidated by this.
`run_cli2.py` also waits for the child before draining `stderr=PIPE` (L37–40), which can deadlock on a noisy child.
The replacement harness is `benchmark/review_rc3/audit.py`.

## E3: `--typed-huf` audio totals differ by thread count

`kolmo -m balanced --typed-huf` totals 8,821,456 bytes at threads=1 (line 350) and 6,840,066 at threads=10 (line 430)
with the same library hash. `--typed-huf` is documented for `-m fast -T1` only; the 10-thread figure equals plain
`balanced`. Needs explanation and a retest; not used for any claim here.
