# rc3 format2 lane: every stacking gain, the audio node and the video node

16 November 2026. Branch `claude/rc3-format2-20261116`, from `claude/release-rc3-20261116`, with
`claude/kaud-20261116` and `claude/rc3-structure-20261116` merged. Everything below is measured against **rc2's own
modes** (the `kolmo` binary of the base commit, `2207da1`), not against the research baselines.

Not in rc3, with the measured reason: packing representations (never smaller than the leaf rc2 writes, on any of 68
integer-residual leaves in four modes; closest +2.8%), Huffman then LZ4 (S1 H->L, see below), the ST-2 decode-cost
gate (nothing to gate: a per-plane leaf decodes at the speed of its sub-codecs; decode time on the set is unchanged),
and planner scores with codecs 21-23 (`-DKOLMO_F2_PLAN=1`: no block changed its plan on the set, so the planner keeps
rc2's cheaper scoring). The other items of section 4 of `research/STACK-AUDIT.md` are listed in `research/CODECS.md`
with their reasons.

All new frames are **container version 11** (format 2). A frame without codecs 19-23 keeps the version it had, so
most files of the generic sets are byte-identical to rc2. SPEC.md describes every new payload.

## Machine and method

- Cloud VM: 4 vCPU Intel Xeon @ 2.10 GHz (AVX-512), 15 GiB, Linux 6.18, gcc 13.3, `make` default flags
  (`-O3 -march=native -ffp-contract=off`). Both builds were made the same way on this machine.
- `run.py BIN OUT.csv MODES FILES...`: one process per (file, mode), `-T1` for compression and decompression (one
  thread each, `benchmark/rc2/PROTOCOL.md`), two processes at a time (`P=2`) on the 4 vCPUs; wall time of the whole
  process; every output decoded and compared with the input. Times are single runs on a shared VM: treat differences
  under ~20% as noise. Sizes are exact and do not depend on threads (the determinism check covers 1 and 4 threads).
- `report.py DIR` made the tables below from `results/fin_*.csv` (columns: file, mode, bytes, compress s,
  decompress s, round trip). `cmp.py A.csv B.csv` compares two runs.
- Inputs (`fetch.sh`, `make_set.py`, `make_wav.py`; public data, nothing committed):
  - set (26 files, 240 MB): `mr_u16.npy`, `xray_u16.npy` (Silesia mr and x-ray as uint16 arrays); MIT-BIH record 100
    as `mitdb-100.i16`, `-ch0.i16`, `-ch1.i16`, `-ch0.i32`, `ecg_2ch_i16.npy`, record 101 channel 0 as
    `ecg101_ch0_i16.npy`; `year` and `population` columns of OWID CO2 (raw `.i32`/`.i64` and `.npy`); a seeded
    `log_timestamps_ms.i64`; OWID `co2` and 16 energy columns as float64 NPY; seeded `sensor_temp_i32.npy` (random walk)
    and `sparse_i64.npy` (1% non-zero); the two OWID CSVs; all-MiniLM-L6-v2 float32 weights (safetensors, 91 MB);
    Silesia dickens, osdb, nci, samba, xml, sao.
  - WAV (43 files): the 40 ESC-50 clips of rc1/kaud (`bench/kaud/fetch_data.sh`), the 40 joined into one mono file,
    paired into 20 stereo clips, and clip 4 with an odd-sized data chunk and a LIST chunk after it. The music set of
    `KAUD.md` needs `flac` to decode, which this VM does not have; it was not run.
  - Y4M: akiyo, foreman, mobile (CIF, 4:2:0, 300 frames) from media.xiph.org.

## How each item was decided

**Per-plane leaf (21).** The plane-major residual stream of an INT node is coded plane by plane, each plane by the
smallest of the block codec, Huffman, rANS (not at the fast levels; at `hc` only 3% smaller than a non-rANS choice,
as for whole leaves), 22, 23 and stored, and kept only when the whole is smaller than rc2's choice for the leaf. The
sparse guard of rc2 applies to every plane through that competition (a mostly-zero high plane goes to zstd, LZMA2 or
23). Small files gain most (ST-8: `year_i32` 1,198 → 793 bytes at fast, `log_timestamps` −19% to −25%).

**Huffman then zstd (22).** The S1 stack as a leaf. zstd 19 runs directly on Huffman streams up to 64 KiB; on larger
ones a probe at the leaf's own zstd level (up to 9; else level 1) runs first, and zstd 19 only when the probe finds at
least 1% of repeats and comes within 5% of the best coding. Without the probe, MiniLM at `eco`/`max` took 6.5 → 16.3 s
and 8.1 → 25.2 s to encode for no gain; with it, 7.1 s and 9.4 s. Starting from zstd at the leaf's level only (an
earlier version) lost the `year_i32` gain; starting from level 1 everywhere lost 7.5 KB on MiniLM at fast.

**Zero-run leaf (23).** Runs of zeros before each non-zero byte (LEB128) and the non-zero bytes, each sub-stream with
the best sub-codec. Tried on leaves and planes that are at least half zero.

**Huffman then LZ4 (S1 H->L), not in.** An encoder-only build with LZ4-HC in place of zstd inside 22 (sizes exact,
frames not decodable) against the final 22: see `results/attribution/att_hl2.csv` (sizes only). In place of zstd, LZ4-HC made the leaf larger on every file where either version applies
(9 file/mode pairs: +0.003% on MiniLM at hc to +23% on `year_i32` at fast) and smaller on none, so H->L adds nothing
next to 22 (as S1 found, +3.78% vs H->Z).

**Packing, not in.** A research build computed, for every INT residual leaf with byte planes, the exact size of
bit packing per 128 values at the block's maximum width and of patched packing (best width per block, exceptions at
1 + width bytes) — 68 leaves over the set in fast/eco/hc/max. None was smaller than the leaf rc2 writes; the closest
was +2.8% (an ECG plane at eco); most were 1.5× to 60× larger (`results/attribution/packing_sizes.txt`). SQ-3 had
already shown packing before zstd enlarges.

**Plane-aligned chunks.** Alone (format 2 off, `-DKOLMO_ALIGNED_LEAVES=1`) they made one file of the set smaller in
every mode (`log_timestamps`: fast −17.1%, eco −19.8%, hc −19.3%, max −21.5%; `results/attribution/al.csv`). With
21-23 on, codec 21 takes those leaves and is smaller still; aligned chunks then add 13 bytes at `hc` and nothing in
the other modes, so they are the default at `hc` only (no format change; encoder time the same within noise).

**Planner scores.** The planner scores candidate graphs with the zstd leaf rule. Scoring with codecs 21-23 too
(`-DKOLMO_F2_PLAN=1`) changed no block on the set, in any mode; rc3 keeps rc2's scores.

**Audio (19).** kaud (profile 0 up to `hc`, profile 1 at `max`/`ultra`) codes each block that holds more than 1024
whole sample frames; the block's other bytes (WAV header, odd byte, trailing chunks) travel as KAU1 head and tail, so
a block may start or end anywhere. It is kept when smaller than the block coded otherwise. That comparison path keeps
rc2's leaf rule: with the format-2 leaf trials on, WAV blocks took 9.7 → 25.8 s at `hc` (kaud itself: 0.45 s for
17.6 MB) and kaud was smaller anyway. Decode is slower than rc2 on WAV: the 43 files (53 MB) decode in 0.73 s at fast (rc2 0.21 s)
and 5.9 s at `max` (profile 1, ~9 MB/s per thread; rc2 1.8 s).

**Video (20).** The whole file's kvid layout (predictor per 16-row strip: MED, previous frame, MED of the difference)
is compressed as a nested Kolmo frame with the caller's mode and threads; the outer frame has one block. Taken in
every mode but `rt` (measured smaller in all four modes on all three clips; `rt` keeps LZ4 speed). A range read
decodes the whole file. Decode at `max` is faster than rc2 (9.4 → 5.6 s for the three clips). The nested frame may not
itself be a video frame (checked; a crafted frame of that shape is refused by `tests/test_format2.c`).

Y4M, and a nested video frame. `tests/kolmotest.c`: graph frames may now be version 7 or 11.

## What failed or was not done

- The kaud music set (needs `flac`) and FFV1/FLAC rivals were not run here; rival comparisons are in `KAUD.md` and
  the rc2 tables. The integration lane repeats the key runs on the Apple M5.
- kaud's `xmalloc` aborts on out-of-memory instead of returning an error (inherited from the tool); decoder
  allocations are bounded by the block size, so only a real out-of-memory reaches it. To be replaced when the memory
