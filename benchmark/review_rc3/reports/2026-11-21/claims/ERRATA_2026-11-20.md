## E1. W4 bwt "inverse BWT +33% (1T)"
- **Source:** 5ce318ad `RESULTS.md:15`, measured on enwik8 on Linux.
- **Fresh result** (5ce318ad and C1 vs rc3, ratio preset): decode speed ties on 7/7 screening files and 21/21 holdout files (gm 0.98–1.00×). Encode is 1.087× gm in screening and ties on the holdout.
- **Status:** NOT REPRODUCED on macOS/arm64. The change relies on MADV_HUGEPAGE, which is Linux only.
- **What does reproduce:** decode peak RSS is 6–20% lower on BWT-routed files.

## E2. W3 decmap "text eco/hc decode +14.8% (1T)"
- **Source:** 59f117f1 `benchmark/rc3w3/decmap/RESULTS.md:15`.
- **Fresh result:** a17d6657 vs rc3 ties on default eco/hc (screening 4/4, holdout 21/21). With KOLMO_NATIVE=all and all,strong it ties on all 18 files (br-decmap-native, gm 0.993–1.007).
- **Status:** NOT RESOLVED on this Mac. Default eco/hc frames on text contain no KHUF/KLZH blocks, so the changed decoders are reached only through KOLMO_NATIVE. Decodes take 11–57 ms, with ±10% CI. The AVX2 word decoder is x86 only.
- No regression was found.

## E3. Ultra (-x) memory
The RC3 materials do not state the ultra memory cost. Fresh measurement on 8 files:
- rc3 `-x` and `-x -l 2` peak at about 3,046 MiB (encode) and about 2,535 MiB (decode);
- W4 cm `-x` peaks at 225 MiB.

Any ultra size or speed claim must also state this memory.

## E4. Rival margins on unseen data
Screening-corpus margins overstate the margins on unseen files:

| comparison | screening | holdout (21 unseen files) |
|---|---|---|
| ratio vs xz-9 | 21.5% smaller | 11.3% smaller |
| hc vs zstd-19 | 19.6% smaller | 16.0% smaller |
| balanced vs zstd-3 | 22.3% smaller | 21.5% smaller |

Speed vs lz4-1 is a size tie on both corpora (−0.26% and +0.25%). Cite holdout figures for general claims.

## E5. eco encode memory
On the holdout, eco's encode peak reaches 1,079 MiB, on the 47 MB weights file and the video files. This differs from the about 720 MiB maximum seen in screening. Claims of low memory for eco should be qualified.

## E6. Domain-specialist claims
Kolmo's claims against FLAC, JPEG XL, FFV1, CRAM/fqzcomp, Pcodec and OpenZL remain **NEEDS_RETEST**: these tools are unavailable on this Mac (`../tool-inventory/TOOL_INVENTORY.md`). No fresh measurement supports or refutes them.
