## Amplification is scoped, not a licence to cherry-pick

R2 D full: 5.24% total saving, 13.20% on four applicable multi-byte streams. R2 C real: 1.26% total, 9.61% on three applicable streams. Keep all no-op controls in the headline. Weight improvements by affected bytes/time: a 10% leaf improvement affecting 20% of file cost is about 2% whole-file improvement, absent other interactions. Page, cache or network-packet thresholds can cause nonlinear application effects, but no such effect was measured here; benchmark the actual threshold before claiming it.

For new validation freeze the entire pipeline and profile, not only components. Audit every release/benchmark manifest and the observed-source register; reserve at least two applicable independent families and dense/single-byte controls. Do not combine one favorable family from different old experiments post hoc and call that an independently validated conjunction.

## Literature controls, not borrowed benchmark results

- OpenZL custom formats describes semantic stream separation and training/clustering: https://openzl.org/getting-started/examples/c/custom-formats/
- zstd's format explicitly signals repeated Huffman/FSE tables, unlike an assumption that table reuse is automatically free or compatible: https://github.com/facebook/zstd/blob/dev/doc/zstd_compression_format.md

These support candidate mechanisms only. They are not Kolmo performance evidence, a licence audit or approval to add dependencies or change formats.
