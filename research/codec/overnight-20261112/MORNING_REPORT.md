## Layered, conditional and learning outcomes

- **Cost-enabling stack:** Type-first detection + fast identity-preserving predictor/inverse + plane layout + per-plane Huffman moves development inputs from production-fast **0/8** smaller-and-feasible to **7/8** in P2-E3, replicated on actual full CLI and in production frames (P2-E12/E14). The guard prevents large regressions (e.g. categorical cover class +46.6% to parity) but its trial/probe cost is real.
- **Actual interaction ablation, R2:** On 11 development full streams native baseline **337,195 B**, aligned HUF alone **322,880**, aligned rANS alone **319,712**, combined **319,539 B**. The H+R benefit overlaps by **14,142 B**; **do not sum 4.245% + 5.185%**. Singleton acceptance alone saves zero real bytes, but enables rANS on a synthetic constant-plane mechanism: R alone **641,236 B**, S alone **628,234 B**, R+S **595,667 B** (against 628,234 baseline). This is a verified enabling interaction, **not** a fresh-input product win. Re-trying the native original plus HRS costs **1.63–1.67×** encoding; fixed HRS avoids that expense.
- **Additional conditional evidence:** E02G per-plane plus sparse-leaf guard rescues a sparse plane while the guard alone changes no bytes; dense-data decode regressions remain. R4 singleton fast path leaves the CollegeMsg frame at **70,850 B** and improves its warm full decode **0.901× / 0.914×**, but pooled seven-RANS-input full decode is **1.015× / 1.006×** (slightly worse). R4 is **not** another compression percentage or a universal speed improvement.
- **Learning was evaluated only as offline alternatives, not deployed:** P2-E15 size-threshold rules and P2-E16 leave-one-family-out depth-1/depth-2/one-nearest-neighbor scoring on development traces did **not** beat fixed tp_phuf_pg16 in both runs.
